In a crisis or need urgent help? Clear Thoughts Psychiatry does not provide emergency or after-hours crisis services. If you are experiencing a psychiatric emergency, call 911 or the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. You can also text NJ to 741741 for the Crisis Text Line, or call 201-262-4357 (262-HELP) for Bergen County mobile crisis response.
