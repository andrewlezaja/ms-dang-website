Insurance & Fees

Accessible, transparent psychiatric care

We accept many major insurance plans and offer self-pay options for individuals without coverage or who prefer to receive care without using insurance benefits.

At Clear Thoughts Psychiatry Inc., we believe accessing quality mental health care should be simple, transparent, and stress-free.

Our team is committed to helping you understand your insurance benefits, expected costs, and available treatment options before beginning care. Because insurance plans and benefits vary, we recommend contacting our office to verify your specific coverage before your first appointment.

Accepted Insurance Plans

In-network insurance plans

Clear Thoughts Psychiatry Inc. is proud to participate with a variety of insurance providers. Current participating plans as of April 28, 2026:

Aetna AARP Blue Cross Blue Shield of New Jersey Cigna Humana Luminare Health Medicare Meritain Health Oxford Health Plans UMR 1199SEIU Anthem / Empire Plans

Additional insurance plans may be added in the future. Please contact our office to confirm whether your specific plan is accepted and to verify your benefits.

Insurance Verification

Before your first appointment, our team can help you understand your coverage and expected patient responsibility. Verification may include:

  • Confirming active insurance coverage
  • Reviewing copays, deductibles, and coinsurance requirements
  • Clarifying mental health benefits
  • Discussing any expected out-of-pocket costs

Please note that verification of benefits does not guarantee payment by your insurance company. Final coverage decisions are determined by your individual insurance plan.

Out-of-Network Options

If we are not in-network with your insurance plan, you may still choose to receive care as a self-pay patient. If your plan includes out-of-network mental health benefits, we can provide documentation or submit claims when appropriate to assist with reimbursement.

  • Payment is collected at the time of service.
  • Insurance reimbursement is determined by your insurance company.
  • Reimbursement amounts vary based on your individual plan benefits.
  • Patients are responsible for any remaining balance not covered by insurance.
Self-Pay Options

Transparent rates, no surprises

For patients without insurance coverage — or those who prefer not to use insurance — self-pay options are available. Choosing self-pay may provide additional privacy and flexibility, as insurance companies are not involved in determining medical necessity or treatment documentation requirements.

Self-pay rates for psychiatric appointments
Appointment Type Length Rate
Initial Psychiatric Evaluation 60 minutes $300
Medication Follow-Up 20 minutes $120
Extended Follow-Up 45 minutes $150

Payment is due at the time of your appointment and processed automatically for your convenience. A superbill is available upon request for submission to your insurance company for potential out-of-network reimbursement. Please contact our office to confirm current self-pay rates and payment options.

Appointment Policy

Cancellation & no-show policy

Your appointment time is reserved exclusively for you. Please provide at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule.

Appointments canceled with less than 24 hours' notice, or appointments missed without prior notification, may be subject to a $50 cancellation/no-show fee.

This fee is the responsibility of the patient and is generally not covered by insurance. The fee must be paid before future appointments may be scheduled, unless alternative arrangements are approved by Clear Thoughts Psychiatry Inc.

We understand that unexpected emergencies and circumstances may occur. Exceptions may be considered at the provider's discretion.
