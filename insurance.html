Accepted Insurance Plans

In-Network Insurance

We participate with select plans from the following insurance providers:

Aetna AARP Blue Cross Blue Shield of New Jersey Cigna Humana Luminare Health Medicare Medicaid Meritain Health Oxford Health Plans UMR 1199SEIU Anthem / Empire Plans

Don't see your plan? We participate with additional insurance plans not listed here, and our network continues to grow. Contact our office to ask about your plan.

Insurance participation may vary by specific plan, network, provider, and service location. Please contact our office to confirm your coverage before scheduling.

Insurance Verification

Before your first appointment, our office can help review:

  • Active insurance coverage
  • Copays, deductibles, and coinsurance
  • Outpatient mental health benefits
  • Estimated patient responsibility

Verification of benefits does not guarantee payment. Final coverage and reimbursement decisions are determined by your insurance plan.

Out-of-Network Options

If your insurance plan is not accepted, you may still choose to receive care through our self-pay option.

A superbill can be provided upon request for possible reimbursement through out-of-network benefits. Reimbursement is not guaranteed and depends on your individual insurance plan.

Appointment Policy

Cancellation & No-Show Policy

Your appointment time is reserved specifically for you. Please provide at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule.

Appointments canceled with less than 24 hours' notice or missed without notification may be subject to a $50 fee, where permitted by applicable law and insurance agreements.

Exceptions may be considered for emergencies or extenuating circumstances, consistent with practice policy.
