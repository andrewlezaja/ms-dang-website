Our Services

Psychiatric Care Built Around You

From your initial psychiatric evaluation to ongoing medication management, Clear Thoughts Psychiatry offers comprehensive outpatient care to help you better understand your symptoms, explore treatment options, and make informed decisions about your mental health.

Psychiatric Evaluations Medication Management Follow-Up Care Telepsychiatry Supportive Care Advanced Referrals
Service 01

Psychiatric Evaluations

Comprehensive assessments that explore your symptoms, psychiatric and medical history, current medications, and treatment goals to guide appropriate recommendations.

During your initial appointment, we also take time to understand your family history and lifestyle, so we can reach an accurate diagnosis and build a treatment plan around your individual needs.

Our goal is to help you better understand your mental health and identify the treatment options that best support your recovery and overall well-being.

Service 02

Medication Management

Individualized psychiatric medication treatment, including medication selection, dose adjustments, education, and monitoring for effectiveness and potential side effects.

During follow-up appointments, we will:

  • Review your symptoms and overall progress
  • Monitor medication effectiveness
  • Evaluate potential side effects
  • Make medication adjustments when clinically appropriate
  • Provide education about your medications and treatment plan
  • Answer your questions and support informed decision-making

We believe medication management is a collaborative process. Every treatment decision is made with your goals, preferences, and overall health in mind.

A note on controlled substances.

We prescribe controlled medications when clinically appropriate and supported by a thorough evaluation and diagnosis. Lab work or additional assessments may be requested before initiating or continuing certain prescriptions.

For safety and continuity of care, we do not automatically continue medications prescribed by other providers.

Service 03

Follow-Up Care

Ongoing appointments to assess progress, review symptoms, address concerns, and make treatment adjustments when appropriate.

Symptom Assessment

Structured review of how you're feeling and functioning since your last visit.

Medication Review

Effectiveness, tolerability, and adjustments made thoughtfully over time.

Treatment Goals

Revisiting what matters most to you and recalibrating the plan as needed.

Care Coordination

Working with your primary care provider or therapist, with your consent.

Patient Education

Clear explanations of your diagnosis, options, and what to expect next.

Ongoing Support

Consistent, reliable follow-through so care doesn't stall between visits.

Our goal is to help you achieve lasting improvement while ensuring your treatment continues to meet your evolving needs.

Service 04

Telepsychiatry

Secure virtual psychiatric appointments for eligible patients physically located in New Jersey or New York at the time of care.

Video visits are HIPAA-compliant and available for both evaluations and follow-up appointments.

Service 05

Supportive Care

Supportive, therapeutic conversations and practical guidance incorporated into psychiatric treatment to help patients navigate emotional challenges and treatment-related concerns.

Supportive care helps patients:

  • Better understand their mental health condition
  • Develop healthy coping strategies
  • Navigate life stressors and transitions
  • Improve emotional resilience
  • Stay engaged with their treatment plan

Therapy referrals

Supportive care is incorporated into psychiatric visits. Patients who would benefit from ongoing psychotherapy are encouraged to work with a licensed therapist. For specialized therapy, we are happy to coordinate care and refer to trusted therapists in the community:

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)
  • Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)
  • Trauma-Focused Therapy
  • EMDR
  • Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP)
Service 06

Advanced Referrals

When clinically indicated, referrals or coordination with outside specialists for services such as specialized psychotherapy, psychological testing, or advanced psychiatric treatments, including:

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS)

A non-invasive treatment using magnetic pulses to stimulate areas of the brain involved in mood regulation.

Ketamine-Based Therapy

Administered by specialty providers for certain treatment-resistant mood conditions when other approaches have not provided adequate relief.

Electroconvulsive Therapy (ECT)

A well-established treatment for severe or treatment-resistant depression and certain other psychiatric conditions.

These treatments are not provided directly within our practice. We collaborate with experienced specialists and help coordinate referrals to ensure continuity of care.
