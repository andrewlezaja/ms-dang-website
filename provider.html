A Note From Rupi

Welcome to Clear Thoughts Psychiatry

Seeking mental health care is a meaningful step toward improving your overall well-being. I understand that reaching out for help can feel overwhelming, which is why I strive to create a safe, supportive, and judgment-free environment where you feel heard, respected, and comfortable discussing your concerns.

I provide compassionate, evidence-based psychiatric care for adolescents, adults, and older adults throughout New Jersey and New York. My services include comprehensive psychiatric evaluations, personalized medication management, and ongoing treatment for a wide range of mental health conditions, including anxiety, depression, ADHD, bipolar disorder, PTSD, obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD), trauma-related disorders, insomnia, and other psychiatric concerns.

My goal is to partner with each patient to develop an individualized treatment plan that promotes emotional wellness, resilience, and long-term mental health.

Education & Credentials

I earned my Master of Science in Nursing (MSN) with a specialization as a Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner from Fairleigh Dickinson University in Teaneck, New Jersey.

I am board certified as a Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (PMHNP-BC) and licensed to practice in both New Jersey and New York. Board certification reflects advanced education, clinical training, and expertise in the assessment, diagnosis, and treatment of mental health conditions across the lifespan.

I remain committed to continuing education and lifelong professional development to ensure my patients receive the highest quality, evidence-based psychiatric care.

Clinical Experience

My clinical experience spans a variety of psychiatric settings, including:

  • Inpatient Psychiatry
  • Outpatient Behavioral Health
  • Telepsychiatry
  • Long-Term Care

Working across these diverse settings has given me experience caring for individuals with a broad range of psychiatric conditions and varying levels of clinical complexity. This background enables me to provide thoughtful, comprehensive care that is tailored to each patient's individual needs.

My Approach

Every person's mental health journey is unique

I recognize that your life experiences, relationships, culture, values, medical history, and personal goals all influence your emotional well-being. Rather than taking a one-size-fits-all approach, I take the time to understand your story and work collaboratively with you to develop a treatment plan that aligns with your needs and goals.

I believe that the most effective psychiatric care is built on trust, compassion, open communication, and shared decision-making. My role is to provide guidance, education, and evidence-based treatment while empowering you to take an active role in your care.

My Philosophy

The best psychiatric care begins with listening.

Mental health treatment is about more than managing symptoms — it's about understanding the whole person and helping you achieve lasting wellness. Together, we'll create a personalized treatment plan designed to improve your emotional well-being, enhance daily functioning, and support your long-term mental health goals.

My commitment is to provide care that is:

  • Compassionate
  • Respectful
  • Collaborative
  • Evidence-Based
  • Patient-Centered
  • Culturally Responsive

I speak English, Hindi, and Punjabi, and I strive to create a therapeutic relationship where you feel supported, understood, and empowered throughout every stage of your mental health journey.
