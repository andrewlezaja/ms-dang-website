Meet Your Provider

Rupinder Kaur (“Rupi”), MSN, PMHNP-BC

Board-Certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner

I am a board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner dedicated to providing compassionate, evidence-based psychiatric care to adolescents, adults, and older adults.

I earned my Master of Science in Nursing from Fairleigh Dickinson University and provide psychiatric services in New Jersey and New York.

Clinical Experience

My clinical experience includes outpatient psychiatry, telepsychiatry, inpatient settings, and care for older adults in long-term care environments. These experiences have shaped my understanding of the complex relationship between mental health, physical health, medications, and daily functioning.

My Clinical Approach

I believe effective psychiatric treatment begins with a comprehensive understanding of each patient's needs.

My approach emphasizes careful assessment, thoughtful medication selection, ongoing monitoring, and shared decision-making. I consider medical conditions, potential medication interactions, treatment history, and personal preferences when developing recommendations.

My goal is to help patients feel informed, supported, and actively involved in their care.

Areas of Clinical Focus

Conditions and concerns I treat

  • Depression and mood disorders
  • Anxiety and panic disorders
  • ADHD and attention-related concerns
  • Bipolar disorders
  • Obsessive-compulsive symptoms
  • Sleep-related concerns
  • Psychiatric care for older adults
  • Medication management for complex psychiatric needs

Care That Speaks Your Language

I provide psychiatric care in English, Hindi, and Punjabi, fostering open communication, cultural understanding, and a comfortable environment where you can express your concerns with confidence.

A Note From Rupi

I'm Glad You're Here.

Taking the first step toward mental health care isn't always easy. You may have been struggling for some time, feeling overwhelmed, or simply wondering where to begin. Whatever brings you here, I want you to know that your concerns matter, and you deserve to be heard.

I believe meaningful care begins with a genuine conversation. You don't need to have all the answers or know exactly what to say. My role is to listen, understand what you're experiencing, and work alongside you to explore treatment options that feel right for your needs and goals.

Whether you're beginning your mental health journey or seeking a fresh perspective, I hope you'll find a place where you feel comfortable asking questions, sharing your concerns, and taking steps forward at your own pace.

I look forward to getting to know you and being part of your journey toward better mental health.

Warmly,
Rupi
