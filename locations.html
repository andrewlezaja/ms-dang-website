Telepsychiatry

Convenient Care Across New Jersey & New York

Access psychiatric care from the comfort and privacy of your home through secure, HIPAA-compliant video appointments.

Telepsychiatry is available for eligible patients physically located in New Jersey or New York at the time of their appointment.

Benefits of Telepsychiatry

  • Convenient virtual appointments
  • Flexible scheduling options
  • Reduced travel time
  • Private, secure video visits
  • Consistent follow-up and medication management

Whether you're beginning treatment or continuing care, telepsychiatry offers a convenient way to stay connected with your psychiatric provider.
