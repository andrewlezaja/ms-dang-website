What Happens Next

Three steps to your first appointment

1

We reach out

A member of our team contacts you within one business day to answer questions and confirm a time that works for you.

2

You complete intake

You'll receive secure intake forms covering your medical history and current medications so your provider can prepare for your visit.

3

Your evaluation

A 60-minute comprehensive psychiatric evaluation, in person or by secure video, resulting in a personalized treatment plan.

In a crisis or need urgent help? Clear Thoughts Psychiatry does not provide emergency or after-hours crisis services. If you are experiencing a psychiatric emergency, call 911 or the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. You can also text NJ to 741741 for the Crisis Text Line, or call 201-262-4357 (262-HELP) for Bergen County mobile crisis response.
